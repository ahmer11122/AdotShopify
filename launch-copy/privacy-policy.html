What we collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and delivery address.

How we use your details

We use your details only to process and deliver your order, confirm it with you, and answer your questions. We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

We share your delivery details only with the courier company that delivers your order.

Payments

If you pay by bank transfer, Easypaisa, or JazzCash, we only use the payment details to confirm your payment.

Emails and updates

If you sign up for our emails, we send you news about new drops. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Keeping your details safe

We keep your details safe and only keep them for as long as we need them for orders and records.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, change, or delete your details. Contact us on WhatsApp +92 370 6735800 or email support@adotoffical.com.

Changes to this policy
