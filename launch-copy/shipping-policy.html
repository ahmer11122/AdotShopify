Where we deliver

We deliver all over Pakistan. We do not ship outside Pakistan right now.

How long it takes

  • Order processing: 1–2 working days after your order is confirmed.
  • Delivery time:
    • Big cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, and similar): 3–5 working days
    • Other cities and towns: 5–7 working days
    • Far or remote areas: up to 10 working days

Working days are Monday to Saturday. Public holidays are not counted. These times are an estimate. Sale seasons, bad weather, strikes, or courier delays can make it take longer.

How much it costs

  • Delivery is PKR 250 for orders under PKR 5,000.
  • Delivery is free for orders of PKR 5,000 and above.
  • You see the exact amount at checkout before you pay.

Courier and tracking

We send orders with trusted Pakistani couriers. When your order ships, we send you the tracking number by WhatsApp, SMS, or email.

Cash on Delivery (COD)

  • You pay cash to the rider when you receive your parcel.
  • There is no extra COD fee.
  • After you order, we may call or WhatsApp you to confirm. If we cannot reach you within 48 hours, we may cancel the order.
  • If a parcel is refused again and again, we may turn off COD for that phone number or address.

Address and phone number

Please give a full and correct address and a phone number that works. The courier will call before delivery. If you gave a wrong address, tell us on WhatsApp as soon as you can. We cannot change the address after the order has shipped.

Delivery attempts

The courier usually tries to deliver more than once. If nobody can receive the parcel, it may be sent back to us.

Damaged parcel

If the outer packing is badly torn or open when it arrives, take a photo before you accept it, and message us on WhatsApp right away.
