Where we deliver

We deliver all over Pakistan. We do not ship outside Pakistan.

How long it takes

  • We process your order within 24 hours.
  • Delivery takes 3 to 4 working days (Monday to Friday).
  • Delivery to remote areas may take longer.
  • Public holidays and bad weather can cause delays.

Shipping charges

  • Orders under Rs. 6,000: Rs. 250 shipping fee.
  • Orders of Rs. 6,000 and above: FREE shipping.
  • You will see the exact amount at checkout before you pay.

Payment methods

  • Cash on Delivery: pay cash when your parcel arrives.
  • Bank transfer, Easypaisa, or JazzCash.
  • There is no extra fee for Cash on Delivery.
  • We may call or WhatsApp you to confirm your order before we send it.

Tracking

When your order ships, we send you a tracking number by email or WhatsApp.
