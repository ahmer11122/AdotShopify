We want you to be happy with your ADOT Official order. Please read this policy before you buy.

Exchange (size or colour)

  • You can ask for an exchange within 7 days after your parcel is delivered.
  • The item must be unworn, unwashed, and have all tags on. It must come back in its original packing.
  • Exchange is for a different size or colour of the same item, and only if we have it in stock.
  • If we do not have your size, you can choose another item of the same price, or ask for store credit.
  • One exchange per order.
  • For a size or change-of-mind exchange, you pay the courier charge to send the item back to us. We pay the courier charge to send the new item to you.

Wrong, damaged, or faulty items

If you get the wrong item, a damaged item, or an item with a fault:

  • Message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Send clear photos. A short unboxing video helps a lot. Please start recording before you open the parcel.
  • We will check and then send a free replacement or give you a full refund. In this case, we pay the courier charge.

Sale items

Items bought in a sale can be exchanged for size only (if in stock). We do not give refunds on sale items, except if the item is wrong, damaged, or faulty.

What we cannot accept

  • Items that are worn, washed, changed, or have no tags.
  • Damage that happened after delivery.
  • Requests after the 7-day window (or after 48 hours for wrong or damaged items).

Refunds

  • Refunds are only for wrong, damaged, or faulty items, or if we cannot give you a replacement.
  • After we receive and check the item, we send your money within 5–7 working days.
  • For COD orders, we refund by bank transfer, JazzCash, or Easypaisa. We will ask you for your account details on WhatsApp.
  • Delivery charges are not refunded, except when the mistake is ours.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel before the order ships. Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible. After it ships, we cannot cancel it. You can still use the exchange rules above.

How to start an exchange or refund

  1. Message us on WhatsApp: +92 370 6735800.
  2. Send your order number, your name, and a photo of the item.
  3. Tell us what you need (size, colour, or the problem).
  4. We reply with the next step and the return address.

Please do not send an item back before we say yes. We cannot take responsibility for parcels sent without approval.
