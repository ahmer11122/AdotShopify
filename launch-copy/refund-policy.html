Size or colour exchange

  • You can exchange within 7 days of delivery.
  • The item must be unworn, unwashed, and have its tags on.
  • We only exchange for a different size or colour, and only if it is in stock.
  • If your size is not available, we will offer you another item of the same price.
  • You can exchange once per order.
  • Sale items cannot be exchanged.
  • For a size or colour exchange, you pay the courier charge to send the item back to us. We send the new item to you.

Wrong, damaged, or faulty item

  • Message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Send clear photos and a short unboxing video. Start recording before you open the parcel.
  • We send a replacement right away and cover the return shipping.

We do not offer refunds. We help with exchanges and replacements as described above.

How to start an exchange

  1. Message us on WhatsApp: +92 370 6735800
  2. Send your order number and your name.
  3. Tell us the size or colour you want, or describe the problem.
  4. Send us the item only after we confirm. We will tell you where to send it.
